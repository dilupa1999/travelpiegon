About The Ritz Paris

Founded in 1898 by César Ritz in collaboration with legendary chef Auguste Escoffier, The Ritz Paris stands as a timeless symbol of French elegance, opulence, and architectural grandeur overlooking the historic Place Vendôme.

Featuring 142 lavishly decorated rooms and suites named after iconic guests such as Coco Chanel, Ernest Hemingway, and F. Scott Fitzgerald, the property blends classic 18th-century French decor with state-of-the-art modern amenities, private terraces, and bespoke butler assistance.
