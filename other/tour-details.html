Parisian Escapes

Tour Overview

Immerse yourself in the world's most romantic capital with Travexa's signature 5-day curated itinerary. Designed for discerning travelers, this tour blends VIP skip-the-line access to iconic monuments like the Eiffel Tower and Louvre Museum with intimate walking tours through bohemian Montmartre and sunset cruises along the Seine River.

You'll stay in handpicked 4-star boutique accommodations in central Paris, enjoy daily chef-curated breakfasts, and travel with expert multilingual local guides throughout your journey.

Tour Itinerary

Welcome to Paris! Your private driver greets you at Charles de Gaulle Airport and transfers you to your 4-star hotel. In the evening, board a luxury glass-topped boat for a 2-hour sunset cruise along the Seine River with champagne.

Enjoy a guided masterpiece tour of the Louvre Museum including Mona Lisa and Venus de Milo. After lunch at a French bistro, ascend to the summit floor of the Eiffel Tower for panoramic city vistas.

Travel back in time to the Royal Palace of Versailles. Explore the breathtaking Hall of Mirrors, King's Grand Apartments, and sprawling fountain gardens with a expert royal historian.

Discover the cobblestone streets of Montmartre, Sacré-Cœur Basilica, and artist studios. Finish the day with a sommelier-led French wine and artisanal cheese tasting session.
